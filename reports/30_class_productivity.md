## Reading

- The productivity law is class-universal: every class grows a steady 2.0-2.6%/yr with log-linear R² 0.93-0.99. Classes differ in LEVEL (a pickup's physics), barely in RATE.
- Car SUV is the fastest (2.57%/yr): the crossover category absorbed hybridization hardest (RAV4/CR-V-class hybrids), and its early-years series is the noisiest (R² 0.93, tiny 1970s-80s volumes).
- Pickups are slowest (2.00%/yr) yet still doubled-plus on constant design.
- No 'coupe' class exists in EPA Trends: two-door cars fold into Sedan/Wagon, and no weight/HP time series exists for two-doors in any project source, so a coupe line is not constructible without fabrication.
- Stage-29 caveats all apply (elasticity stability, EV mix in recent years -- especially the Car SUV line, log-linear extrapolation).

## EV productivity (fig44)

- Series: EPA Trends Tesla rows, 2012-2024 (n = 13), the only production-weighted EV series with weight in any project source; the catalog has MPGe but no weight/HP.
- Normalized (weight elasticity -0.975 from the BEV companion model): **+2.36%/yr, R² = 0.716** -- inside the same 2.0-2.6%/yr band as every combustion class. One clock, two energy carriers.
- 2045 at trend: 207 MPGe-equivalent at constant 2024 Tesla weight.
- Caveats: Tesla is a one-manufacturer proxy for 'EV'; model-mix shifts (S/X -> 3/Y) drive part of the path; 13 points; no HP control (the BEV model has none).
