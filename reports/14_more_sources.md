## Credit-story audit (the fig19 honesty standard)

Loan rate vs truck share of production, 1972-2024:

| Specification | slope | R^2 | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Levels | -0.0296 | 0.568 | 2.7e-10 |
| First differences | -0.0062 | 0.045 | 0.14 |

Same verdict as gas prices: the levels relationship is the secular-trend illusion (rates fell for four decades while truck share rose for five), and the differenced relationship is indistinguishable from noise. The credit story joins the gas-price story in the 'seductive overlay, no annual association' bin. Present as revision #7, not as a cause.
