- Footprint drops out: in spec B its t = -0.14 (p = 0.89); nested F[1, 1279] = 0.03, p = 0.872. Size travels with weight; once the weight term is in, footprint adds nothing. Spec F is spec B without it.

- Hybrid premium exp(0.2238) - 1 = 25%; BEV premium exp(1.708) - 1 = 452% at identical weight, power, footprint -- the energy-conversion advantage of an electric drivetrain over combustion.
