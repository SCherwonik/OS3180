# Design-Normalized Fuel-Economy Productivity

Generated by 29_productivity.py.

## Construction

MPG_norm(t) = fleet real-world MPG x (weight_t/weight_2024)^0.304 x (HP_t/HP_2024)^0.483, elasticities from the stage-19 model. Interpretation: what each year's technology would have delivered at 2024's fleet weight and power.

## Findings

- The 1987-2004 MPG trough disappears: normalized productivity is monotone (9.4 -> 13.7 -> 17.2 -> 27.2 for 1975/1987/2004/2024). Raw MPG fell in the middle years only because the gains were spent on weight and power.
- Growth is close to exponential and startlingly steady: **1.94%/yr over 1975-2024 (R2 = 0.984)**; 2.27%/yr over 2005-2024 (R2 = 0.985).

## Caveats (present with the chart)

- Elasticities are estimated on the 2024 cross-section and applied to five decades: a stability assumption, partially supported by the era refits (R2 0.71-0.78 across 2000+/2020+ windows).
- Fleet real-world MPG folds EV energy-equivalents in, so part of post-2011 'productivity' is electrification mix, not combustion technology; the projection inherits that blend and effectively assumes the electrification pace continues.
- Straight-line extrapolation in log space: no physics ceiling, no policy scenario. The two rates bracket, not bound, the future.
- Slope-CI band on the chart reflects fit uncertainty only, not scenario uncertainty, which dominates.
