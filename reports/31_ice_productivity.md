# Combustion-Only Productivity (figs 45-46, ex-BEV twins of 42-43)

Generated by 31_ice_productivity.py.

## Method

BEV share s backed out per year and class: MPG_ice = (1-s) / (1/MPG_all - s/MPGe_bev) (harmonic, gallons-first, matching EPA's aggregation); weight and HP removed arithmetically. BEV proxy = EPA Trends Tesla series (production-weighted, same real-world basis). Hybrids and PHEVs remain included by design.

## Fleet (fig45)

- Normalized combustion productivity: **+1.86%/yr over 1975-2024 (R² = 0.984)**; 2005-2024 rate +1.87%/yr.
- Projection at constant 2024 design: 39 by 2045 at the recent rate, 39 at the 50-year rate.
- Versus fig42 (BEVs in): the 50-year rate barely moves (electrification is late and fleet share small), but the recent-rate projection drops -- that gap IS the electrification contribution to measured fleet productivity.

## Caveats

- Tesla as the all-BEV proxy: adequate at fleet level (BEV <= 7.2%), coarser inside Car SUV 2023 (35.7% share, so proxy error is first-order there); non-Tesla BEVs average lower MPGe, meaning the true ICE line would sit very slightly higher.
- Harmonic removal assumes EPA's gallons-first aggregation, per their methodology.
- All stage-29 caveats (elasticity stability, log-linear extrapolation) still apply.
