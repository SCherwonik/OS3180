## Caveats

- 2015-2020: the early-EV era. BEV medians here reflect Bolt/Leaf/Tesla-S/X pricing, not today's market; the 2026 acquisition-cost picture is fig37 (KBB ATP).
- MSRP is the asking price; transactions (fig37) include incentives and mix.
- Trim-level medians: luxury brands list many trims, so these are catalog medians, not sales-weighted prices.
- Sample feed is free for evaluation; cite as 'CarAPI vehicle data feed, free sample, accessed 2026-09-10'.
