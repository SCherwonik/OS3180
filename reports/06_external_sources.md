Caveats:

- Calendar year vs model year: model-year N vehicles sell across calendar N-1..N. Joining calendar context to model-year fleet data is an approximation; do not read single-year wiggles as causal, read multi-year swings.
- Gas price series starts 1976 (unleaded); 1975 has no value.
- CAFE standard values are the passenger-car national standards; light trucks had separate, lower standards not included here. Compare the standard ONLY against `cafe_2cycle_mpg` (same test basis), never against real-world MPG.
- `new_vehicle_real_price_index_1975` uses the BLS new-vehicle CPI, which is hedonically quality-adjusted: its decline means price per unit of quality fell, NOT that sticker prices fell. Do not present it as 'cars got cheaper' without that framing.
