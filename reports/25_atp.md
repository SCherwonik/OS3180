## Limitations

- ATP is the average price paid for vehicles actually sold: it mixes list price with sales mix and incentives, and is not an MSRP average across the catalog.
- **No hybrid-only ATP exists in these reports**: Cox's ICE+ bucket folds hybrids in with gasoline. The three-way BEV/Hybrid/Gasoline price split the energy-cost analysis uses cannot be completed from public KBB data; stated rather than estimated.
- Single month (July 2026). Cox publishes monthly; a time series would need the article archive walked backward.
- Tesla model figures are published approximations.
