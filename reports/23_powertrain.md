# BEV vs Hybrid vs Gasoline

Generated by 23_powertrain_compare.py. One row per (model year, powertrain group) in outputs/powertrain_trends.csv.

## Definitions and exclusions

- Groups: BEV (fuelType1 = Electricity), FCEV (Hydrogen), Hybrid (non-plug-in), Gasoline. FCEV efficiency is MPGe and its cost per mile is left null: no retail hydrogen price series exists in the project's sources. Excluded: PHEV (447 configs; per-mile cost needs utility-factor assumptions), Diesel (1,310), Other (62).
- `median_comb08` is the window-sticker combined value: MPG for gasoline/hybrid, **MPGe for BEV** -- an energy-equivalence conversion (33.7 kWh/gal), not the same physical quantity. Charts must carry that label.
- Cost per mile uses each model year's national average prices (BLS via FRED, from outputs/context_finance.csv): energy only, no purchase price, depreciation, insurance, or DC-fast premiums.
- BEV `median_range_mi` from the catalog's rated range field. Gasoline/hybrid driving range is NOT computable from this catalog (no tank-capacity field); the report says so rather than inventing a figure.
- Average vehicle purchase price by group is not available in any project dataset (fueleconomy.gov has no MSRP; the BLS new-vehicle CPI is a hedonic index, not a level). Left out rather than fabricated.
